# Sector 13

Sector 13 houses an old Archive. Thousands of useless records are created continuously —
disposable identities, repeated entries, noise. Behind all that noise there is hidden data.

Voss left an instruction:

> **Break the Archive.**

The Archive accepts records through `add_records`: each record has a **weight**, the load it adds
to the archive. It is reindexed by `rebuild`, the maintenance pass that walks **every** record and
totals the weights. The door checks each weight on its own; the maintenance works with the total.
The snapshot only answers once the maintenance has stopped working; until then, the system treats
the Archive as intact.

## What to do

> **Break the Archive. Then see what it was hiding.**

```js
await window.contract.stored()
await window.contract.rebuild()
await window.contract.add_records({ weights: [/* weights, numbers */] })
// After breaking the maintenance:
await window.contract.snapshot()
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Read what `add_records` checks on each weight and what `rebuild` does with all of them. Once the maintenance
stops working, call `snapshot` and read its return value. Then click **Submit**.
