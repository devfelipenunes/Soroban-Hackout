## Technical proof confirmed

The Custodian's `enter` was correct: it required your signature, checked the identity's controller, and then followed the chain of links up to the operator identity. The problem was one layer below: `register_identity` required only the signature of whoever was registering, and the link to the **parent** was declared by that same caller, with no agreement from the parent's controller. You registered an identity of your own under the operator's and inherited its authority.

This is a **chain of trust** flaw: a correct contract makes an unsafe decision when it trusts a link the caller was able to write. The defense is to require the parent controller's authorization when the link is created (`parent_controller.require_auth()`).
