# The Dead Man's Wallet

The Vault service trail cites Elias Var, a technician who died six months ago. His legacy profile
still responds, even though the contract was configured when the instance was created.

Inspect the current administrator and find out whether the installation routine can change that
configuration after the instance already exists. The technical record is a clue; ledger data is
public, and call authorization does not make it confidential.

## What to do

> **Find out why a closed identity can still reconfigure the registry.**

Start by observing the initial state. `window.help()` lists the methods available in this case.

```js
await window.contract.admin()
```

Once reconfiguration lets you read the record and release the balance to your wallet, click
**Submit**.
