## Technical proof confirmed

`transfer` correctly authenticated the source address and checked that it had enough balance. The flaw was the write order: on a self-transfer, the debit was written and then overwritten by a credit calculated from the original balance. One call could therefore inflate the balance enough to open the gate.

The fix is to treat `from == to` as a no-op or calculate and write the result atomically. The Custodian’s response and what lies beyond the passage appear in the next opening.
