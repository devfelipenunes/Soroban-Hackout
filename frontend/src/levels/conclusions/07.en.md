The withdrawal is the direct path: `withdraw` uses `wrapping_sub`, and withdrawing
`2³² − impossible()` from a zero balance lands on the target; `withdraw_checked` uses
`checked_sub` and refuses. The product of `stake`, `rate`, and `elapsed` also exceeds the `u32`
domain: `accrue` uses `wrapping_mul`/`wrapping_add` and `accrue_checked` uses `checked_*`. The
defense is `checked_*` plus the balance comparison. The target is derived per player, so one
instance's answer does not work on another.
