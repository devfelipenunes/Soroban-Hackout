## Technical proof confirmed

The Archive door accepted any non-empty record, but the maintenance pass (`rebuild`) required the
full format of every one. A single malformed record was enough to make the rebuild fail **for good**:
nothing removes records, so the maintenance function became unusable. That is a denial of service by
invalid record: an operation denied by data the attacker controls, with no volume involved. The defense
is to validate on write with the same rules used on read, to process in batches that skip or set aside
what is invalid, and to keep a way to remove records.

The validator does not infer this: it calls the maintenance pass and requires the `CorruptRecord`
failure. The external indexer policy and its discovery belong to the narrative continuation in the
next opening.
