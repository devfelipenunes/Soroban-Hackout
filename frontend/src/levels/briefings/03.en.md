# Warehouse 17

The warehouse sits in Lower Meridian, away from the usual transit routes. Officially it is a
deactivated depot. In practice, it keeps receiving equipment.

It is not a criminals' hideout. It is worse: everything there looks legal. Hundreds of stolen
devices are catalogued, transferred, and registered correctly by the system. Not a single
corrupted record.

The object that appeared in the Vault footage is here, under the warehouse's ownership
contract. The contract answers to `owner_of` and exposes
`transfer_item`.

## What to do

> **Steal an object the system believes you are allowed to own.**

```js
await window.contract.owner_of({ item_id: 17 })
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Once item 17 belongs to you, click **Submit**.
