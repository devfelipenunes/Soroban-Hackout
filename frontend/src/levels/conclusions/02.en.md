## Technical proof confirmed

`initialize` could be called again to overwrite administrative authority. Use an atomic
`__constructor` or a separately guarded initialization with appropriate authorization. The reinit
grants `release`, which moves the wallet balance; the record is the clue, not the prize.

On-chain storage is public, including its stored bytes. The teaching instance returns those bytes
through `read_private_record`; authorization controls the call, not data confidentiality. In the
story, an external service trusting the compromised admin may improperly release documents. The
continuation and canonical release of control appear in the next slides.
