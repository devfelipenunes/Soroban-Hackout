# Warehouse 17

Warehouse 17 sits in Lower Meridian and still receives equipment despite being listed as
deactivated. Item 17 appears in the inventory under the contract's own ownership.

An independent dispute questions who is entitled to the equipment. Compare the current owner, the
intended recipient, and the address whose authorization the contract requires for a transfer.

## What to do

> **Make the contract transfer item 17 to you, then determine who authorized the operation.**

Start by checking the recorded owner. `window.help()` lists the methods available in this case.

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

```js
await window.contract.owner_of({ item_id: 17 })
```

Once the record shows you as the owner, click **Submit**.
