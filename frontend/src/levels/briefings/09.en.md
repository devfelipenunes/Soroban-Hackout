# The Oracle

In Meridian, the Oracle is treated as an almost supernatural machine. Police, insurers, and
companies consult it to predict anomalies. It never explains how it reaches an answer.

The contract exposes `current_sequence`, `choices`, `predict`, and
`reveal`. Register **a round of three targets** before the first one; a single `reveal`,
after the last target, checks the sequence. Three hits in the round open the way.

## What to do

> **Predict the Oracle before it predicts you.**

```js
await window.contract.current_sequence()
await window.contract.choices()
await window.contract.streak({ player: window.player })
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Once the round has three hits, click **Submit**.
