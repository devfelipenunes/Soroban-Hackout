## Technical proof confirmed

The Archive door checked each weight on its own, under a ceiling of `2147483647`, but the
maintenance pass (`rebuild`) added every weight into a `u32` without checking for overflow. Two
records, each one valid, pushed the sum past 2³²: the rebuild aborted **for good**, because nothing
removes records and the maintenance function became unusable. That is a denial of service by an
**unchecked sum**: an operation denied by data the attacker controls, every piece valid on its own
and no volume involved. The defense is to validate the aggregate on write as well, to use checked
arithmetic (`checked_add`), and to keep a way to remove records.

The validator does not infer this: it calls the maintenance pass and requires it to
**abort** (a VM trap, with no named error). The external indexer policy and its discovery belong to the narrative continuation in the
next opening.
