# Sector 13

Sector 13 houses an old Archive. Thousands of useless records are created continuously —
disposable identities, repeated entries, noise. Behind the saturation there is hidden data.

Voss left an instruction:

> **Break the Archive.**

The Archive accepts batches through `add_records` and exposes `count`, `stored`,
`threshold`, and `snapshot`. The counter adds every entry; the archive stores unique
records. The snapshot only answers once the counter reaches the limit; until then, the system
treats it as intact.

## What to do

> **Break the Archive. Then see what it was hiding.**

```js
await window.contract.threshold()
await window.contract.count()
await window.contract.stored()
// After filling the counter:
await window.contract.snapshot()
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Once `count` reaches `threshold`, call `snapshot` and read its return value. Compare
`count` with `stored`. Then click **Submit**.
