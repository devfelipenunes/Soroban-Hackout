## Technical proof confirmed

The Warehouse checked that the supplied `owner` matched the recorded owner, but required the
`recipient` to sign. The transfer changed ownership without authorization from the party losing that
right. This is **wrong auth subject**: a signature is present, but it belongs to the wrong party.

The transfer should require `owner.require_auth()` and verify that `owner` is the current owner.
This differs from the previous case: case 02 accepted a new initialization after configuration;
here, the contract asks the recipient to authorize instead of the current owner.

The evidence confirms an improper transfer; it does not legitimize ownership. Custody and the next
lead appear in the following opening.
