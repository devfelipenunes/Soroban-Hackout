# The Custodian

The only entrance to the First Layer is guarded by the Custodian. It is not an enemy with HP:
it is a gate that opens when your balance reaches its required threshold.

The terminal exposes `balance`, `threshold`, `transfer`, `gate_open`, and `enter`.

## What to do

> **Find a way to reach the balance required by the gate.**

```js
await window.contract.balance({ owner: window.player })
await window.contract.threshold()
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Once the gate opens for you, click **Submit**.

Pass `from`, `to`, and `amount` to a transfer call; you start with 1 credit.
