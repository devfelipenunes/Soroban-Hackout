# The Dead Man's Wallet

The Vault service trail cites Elias Var’s identity, a technician who died six months ago.
This is operator metadata, not proof that his wallet received the funds. Investigate why the
legacy technical profile still accepts operations.

The teaching instance exposes `initialize`, `read_private_record`, and
`release`. The first two represent the archive release in the story; `release` moves the
wallet balance to the admin. The balance starts at the contract address; the record points to the
next destination. Authorization controls who calls each method; it does not make the data
confidential.

## What to do

> **Become someone who should no longer exist.**

```js
await window.contract.admin()
await window.contract.read_private_record({ admin: window.player })
await window.contract.release({ admin: window.player })
await window.contract.balance({ who: window.player })
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Once the registry believes you are the admin, the record has been read, and
`balance` shows your wallet's funds, click **Submit**.
