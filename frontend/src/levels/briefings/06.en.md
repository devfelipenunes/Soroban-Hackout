# The Missing Investigator

Aren Voss’s abandoned terminal contains a fragment whose recovery could not be confirmed.
Archive Index and Decoder disagree about processing. Investigate their interaction and prove
the inconsistency. The Decoder address is available in `window.aux`.

## What to do

> **Recover the message the Archive believes it already recovered.**

```js
window.aux
await window.contract.recovered()
await window.contract.rejected()
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Once the recording appears, click **Submit**.
