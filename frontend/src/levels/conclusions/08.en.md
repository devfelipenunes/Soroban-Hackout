## Technical proof confirmed

`add_records` accepts writes without authorization, and each unique record creates persistent
entries. The indexer must read every record to rebuild its index. At 201 records, the call exceeds
the game's limit of 200 persistent reads per transaction, so the host rejects the rebuild. This
demonstrates resource exhaustion for that operation, not an outage of the Stellar network.

Mitigate this by limiting and authorizing growth and rebuilding in pages with bounded work.
Because a rejected transaction rolls back its own writes, `begin_rebuild` records the request
first; the validator checks that the archive is above the limit and no rebuild completed.
