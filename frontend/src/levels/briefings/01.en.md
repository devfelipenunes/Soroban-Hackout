# No Forced Entry

Mara Vale has closed the physical inspection of the Vault. Doors intact, no explosives, no
stolen credentials, no signature attributed to the owner. The report concludes that "the system
was not violated".

You have been given access only to the contract that governs the service door. It answers to
`owner_of` and `is_open`, and exposes `open`.

## What to do

> **Enter the Vault without possessing its key.**

Read the door's owner before anything else. It is not you. Find out who it is — and what the
contract does with that name when you call `open`.

```js
await window.contract.owner_of({ door_id: 17 })
await window.contract.is_open({ door_id: 17 })
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Once `is_open` returns `true`, click **Submit**.
