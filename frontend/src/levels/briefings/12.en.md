## What to do

Open the forensic session and prove the exploit to the independent observer. Qualification measures technical ability; it does not decide whether the intervention was consensual or legitimate.

```js
await window.contract.original()
await window.contract.payload()
await window.contract.executed_payload()
await window.contract.evidence_verified()
await window.contract.eta()
await window.contract.rewritten()
await window.contract.session({ player: window.player })
```

Methods with arguments take one object of named arguments; `window.help()` lists this case's names.

Success is observable: evidence is verified, `original()` remains `DiagnoseCore`, the executed payload targets another owner, and `rewritten()` and `session({ player: window.player })` return `true`.
